The Art of

Joelle Cook

Joelle Cook is an interdisciplinary artist, fiction author, science-curious poet, and transmedia worldbuilder.

Her artwork and poetry is an exploration of symbiosis and relationality in the natural world, highlighting how form follows function in both the humanities and the sciences. Her passions for architecture, art and storytelling have led her to the practice of worldbuilding, creating new universes within her novels and artwork. Joelle creates her designs primarily through drawing and printmaking, then places her animal compositions into new contexts and mediums to allow them to evolve into new iterations.
